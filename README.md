# INFORMATION OFFSET IN TEXT IMAGES: A MULTI-LEVEL FLEXIBLE STYLE-INFORMED SEGMENTATION-AWARE PERSPECTIVE
In complex image scenes, the accurate segmentation and adaptive inpainting of text regions amidst noise and clutter caused by human or environmental factors is vital for downstream applications ranging from document recovery to automated information extraction. However, existing techniques often fall short when dealing with the intricate spatial relationships and varying degradation patterns present in real-world scenarios. In response, this research proposes the novel multi-level and dual-module flexible style-informed segmentation-aware network dubbed MFSSNet for blind text image inpainting that requires no prior knowledge of corruption masks or degradation patterns. Unlike existing methods that either require explicit masks or use hundreds of diffusion steps, MFSSNet achieves single-pass restoration through explicit structure-style decomposition. Our unified architecture (MFSSNet's) key innovations proposes: (1) complex vector representations in state space models enabling phase-invariant feature learning, critical for rotation-robust character modeling; (2) a cross-spatial attention which creates implicit character topology graphs capturing structural dependencies; (3) a hybrid diffusion-deterministic architecture which achieves optimal structure-texture decomposition verified through frequency domain analysis and (4) mixed-domain training improving real-world generalization; with a performance boost attributed to our Character Consistency Loss which preserves fine-grained structural integrity through morphological operations and patch-based similarity. The findings through meticulous experiments underscore the potential of our MFSSNet, which harbours a divide-and-conquer strategy to perform favourably against state-of-the-art algorithms to elevate both segmentation precision and restoration quality.  Moreover, MFSSNet offers a practical solution for handling degraded text in intricate scenes. To ensure research replicability and further refinement, our approach is readily obtainable online at: https://github.com/voxtranslate/MFSSNet.
